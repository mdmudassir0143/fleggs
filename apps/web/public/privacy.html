Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026 (waitlist and approvals)

Flurry Eggs is a fan-made game inspired by Lofi the Yeti, running on the Sui test network.

Signing in

You sign in with Google through zkLogin (provided by Enoki, Mysten Labs). The game asks Google for a sign-in ID and your email address (the openid and email scopes), never your name, photo or contacts. The ID becomes your Sui account address. Your email is used only to check your place on the waitlist (whether you're approved to play); it is stored on the game server next to your account address, visible only to the game admin, and never shared or sold.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store the email address you type, and when you joined, so we can send you an invite. It is only visible to the game admin, never shared or sold, and we won't send anything else. To be removed, open an issue (below) or reply to the invite.

What is public

Your Sui address and everything you do in the game (eggs, yetis, scores, trades, the nickname you choose) are recorded on the Sui blockchain, which is public. Yeti cards and stories are stored on Walrus, also public.

What the game server keeps

Short-lived game run records (to check scores) and request rate limits. No email, no tracking cookies, no ads, no selling of data.

Your browser

Small settings (sound on/off, which guide you have seen, your sign-in session) are kept in your own browser storage. Clearing site data removes them.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on github.com/mdmudassir0143/Flurry-eggs.
